# Elite Education Meta Pro Landing Page

## Deploy
Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` to the landing-page root.

## Before production
- Replace `PIXEL_ID` in `index.html` with the actual Meta Pixel ID.
- Connect the form submission to the CRM/lead API.
- Configure Meta Conversions API server-side.
- Verify the WhatsApp number and message.
- Replace placeholder branding/images with approved Elite Education assets.
- Verify current programme/university availability and eligibility.
- Add Privacy Policy and Terms URLs.
- Test all UTM parameters from a real Meta ad click.
- Test PageView, Lead and Contact events in Meta Events Manager.

## Recommended Meta campaign URL
Use UTMs such as:
?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign={campaign.name}&utm_content={ad.name}&utm_term={adset.name}

## Main conversion
Lead form -> WhatsApp counsellor handoff.

## Secondary conversion
Direct WhatsApp click.
