Frequently asked.

Have a question that isn't here? Send us your details and our team can guide you.

Ask a counsellor →
How do I choose the right programme?

Start with your current qualification, career goal and preferred field. A counsellor can help you compare suitable options.

Can working professionals apply?

Many of the programmes listed by Elite are designed around flexible learning routes. Confirm eligibility and current delivery mode for your chosen programme before applying.

Which universities are available?

Elite currently lists Uttaranchal University, Pondicherry University, Bharathidasan University, IGNOU, Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University and selected additional options. Availability can vary by programme and intake.

Is every programme available in every university?

No. Programme availability, eligibility, mode of study and admission requirements vary. Ask for the current details for your preferred course.
